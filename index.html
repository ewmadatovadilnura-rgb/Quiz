Linking Words Quiz

and · but · so · because · however · when · until

How it works

• 20 questions, 30 seconds each.

• Choose the best word for the gap.

• After every answer you see the correct word and a short explanation.

• Questions come in a new random order each time.

Finished

Quick rule

and = adds information

but / however = contrast

because = reason

so = result

when = time

until = up to a time
