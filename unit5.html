Unit 5 Grammar Quiz

can / can't · was / were / could

How it works

• 20 questions, 30 seconds each.

• Choose the correct answer.

• After every answer you see the correct answer and a short explanation.

• Questions come in a random order each time.

Finished

Quick rules

can / can't = ability now (I can swim)

could / couldn't = ability in the past (I could swim when I was five)

After can / could we use the base verb: She can swim.

was = I, he, she, it · were = you, we, they
